Detailed Process
Intake
🏭
Material Deposit
Loader tips DMR
Waste collection vehicles deliver DMR to the tipping floor. Wheeled loader pushes material onto the Metering Bin.
🗃️
Metering Bin
Controls feed rate
Regulates the rate of material entering the plant. Prevents downstream overloading and ensures consistent throughput.
6 POSITIONS
👷
Pre-Sort Cabin
Manual contaminant removal
6 operatives manually remove items that would damage machinery or contaminate outputs. Critical first quality gate.
Pre-sort outputs
🎬 Film (×2) 🗑 Residue
Screening
⚙️
OCC Screen
3-fraction size separation
Rotating star screen. Large flat cardboard goes OVER (OCC output). Medium + small material falls UNDER and feeds the DRS Screen.
⚙️
DRS Screen
2-fraction split
Receives unders from OCC Screen. Medium material (2D/3D containers, paper) goes OVER → New Sorters. Small fines (glass, metals, shredded paper) fall UNDER → Fines sub-stream.
↑ Overs (medium)
→ New Sorter #1
↓ Unders (fines)
→ Mag Belt → Trommel
OCC Screen overs
📦 OCC / Cardboard → Bunker
Fines Sub-stream
🧲
Magnetic Belt
Ferrous removal from fines
Overhead magnet on the fines line removes ferrous metals (steel fragments) before the Trommel. Prevents metal from contaminating the glass fraction.
🎡
Trommel
3-fraction rotary screen
Rotating perforated drum separates fines into 3 fractions by size: 0–10mm (residue/dust), 10–50mm (glass-rich fraction → ZAC), and +50mm overs (residue).
0–10mm fraction
🗑 Residue / Dust
10–50mm → ZAC System
💨 Glass separation
+50mm overs
🗑 Residue
🌪️
ZAC System
Glass cleaning from 10–50mm
Processes only the 10–50mm Trommel fraction. Separates clean glass from other mixed material in that size range. Glass sent to bunker for dispatch.
ZAC outputs
🍶 Clean Glass → Bunker 🗑 Organic fines
Magnetic Belt output
🥫 Ferrous Collection
2D/3D Separation + Fibre
NEW SORTER #1
⚙️
New Sorter #1
2D/3D initial split
Acts on the medium-fraction overs from the DRS Screen. Screen allows flat 2D material (paper/card) to travel up and over; 3D containers bounce down and forward.
NEW SORTER #2
⚙️
New Sorter #2
2D/3D second pass
Second pass to improve separation efficiency. Captures material not separated by Sorter #1. Increases purity of both 2D and 3D output streams.
⚙️
Polishing Screen
Final 2D/3D refinement
Final mechanical 2D/3D separation. Ensures clean split between fibre stream (going to NRTs #1 & #5) and container stream (going to container sort line).
2D → Fibre line
→ NRT #5 → NRT #1 (Fibre)
3D → Container line
→ Magnetic Belt → NRT #2
NRT #5
🔬
NRT #5
Optical — Fibre
Near-infrared optical sorter on the fibre line. First NRT pass on 2D material. Identifies and ejects target fibre grades using high-speed air jets.
NRT #1
📷
NRT #1
Optical — Fibre
Second NRT pass on the fibre stream. Increases purity of paper and cardboard fractions before the Fibre Cabin quality check.
10 POSITIONS
👷
Fibre Cabin
Quality check + bunker feed
Operatives inspect fibre quality and remove contaminants. Clean fibre is sorted into bunkers: OCC Bunker, N+P (News & Pam) Bunker, Fibre Bunker, Film Bunker.
Fibre cabin outputs → bunkers
📦 OCC 📰 N+P 📄 Fibre 🎬 Film
Container Sort Line
🧲
Magnetic Belt
Ferrous extraction
Overhead magnet removes ferrous steel cans and fragments from the 3D container stream before optical sorting begins.
NRT #2
📷
NRT #2
Optical — PET
NIR optical sorter targeting PET (polyethylene terephthalate). Ejects PET bottles, pots and trays into PET Bunker.
NRT #3
📷
NRT #3
Optical — HDPE
NIR optical sorter targeting HDPE (high-density polyethylene). Milk bottles, detergent bottles and HDPE containers ejected to HDPE Bunker.
🔋
Eddy Current
Aluminium extraction
Rotating magnetic rotor induces eddy currents in aluminium, repelling cans away from the plastic stream. Aluminium sent to Aluminium Bunker.
NRT #4
🔬
NRT #4
Optical — Mixed Plastics
Captures remaining mixed polymer types (PP, PS, etc.) not targeted by NRT #2 or #3. Ejected to Mixed Plastics Bunker.
10 POSITIONS
👷
Container Cabin
Final quality check → bunkers
Final quality gate on the container line. Operatives inspect all container commodities after optical sorting, remove any cross-contamination, then direct clean material to the appropriate bunkers for baling.
Container cabin → bunkers
🧴 PET 🥛 HDPE ♻️ Mixed Plastics 🥫 Steel 🥤 Aluminium
Output & Dispatch
Quality-checked material from both cabins is stored in commodity bunkers. When a bunker is ready, material is offloaded onto the baler infeed chain belt, which feeds the two baler hoppers.

📦 OCC / Cardboard 📰 News & Pam 📄 Mixed Fibre
📦 Lyndex Twin Ram Baler — Containers & Metals
